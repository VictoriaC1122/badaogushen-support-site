PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 13, 2026

Data collection Market data On-device data Notifications Sharing & tracking Your controls Service notice

Information we collect

The current version does not require an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, address, or payment details. The app does not request access to your camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, HealthKit data, or tracking permission.

Market data and network connections

The app and widgets connect to public services operated by the Taiwan Stock Exchange and Taipei Exchange for Taiwan stocks, ETFs, indices, valuation data, trading activity, price history, and corporate actions. U.S. screens connect to Yahoo Finance for delayed daily and historical data covering a curated set of stocks, ETFs, and major indices. Requests do not include your watchlist, paper portfolio, or recently viewed securities.

As with ordinary internet connections, these providers may receive an IP address, User-Agent, and standard connection metadata. Their handling of that information is governed by their respective policies.

  • Taiwan Stock Exchange OpenAPI
  • Taipei Exchange OpenAPI
  • Yahoo Finance

Information stored on your device

Virtual TWD and USD balances, paper orders, positions, results, and order history are stored on your device. Watchlists, recent views, market cache, notification preferences, bookmarks, and reading progress are also stored locally to preserve your state and improve startup speed. This information is not uploaded to our servers and is never sent to a broker or exchange.

Notifications

Closing reminders are optional. The app requests iOS notification permission only after you view an explanation and choose to enable reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device and can be disabled in the app or system settings. Declining permission does not affect any other feature.

Sharing, advertising, and tracking

Stock Mastery contains no advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking SDK, and we do not sell personal data. Apart from network requests required to retrieve the market data described above, local watchlist and usage records are not provided to third parties.

Your data controls

You can reset a paper account or clear watchlist, browsing, and learning records from the Profile screen. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data. iOS notification permission can be changed in system settings.

Service and investment notice

Stock Mastery provides end-of-day market information, paper-trading practice, and general educational material. It is not a broker. A paper order creates only a virtual record on your device, uses no real funds, and is never submitted as a real order. Simulated fills, commissions, taxes, and results are educational estimates and do not include every market condition, exchange rate, regulatory fee, brokerage fee, discount, minimum fee, tax effect, or product exception.

The app does not provide real trading, brokerage account opening, managed investing, personalized investment advice, or guaranteed returns. Taiwan data is end of day. U.S. data covers selected securities and uses delayed daily data; it is not a real-time or complete market feed. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or temporarily replaced by cached information. Confirm any real decision with official exchange and brokerage information.

Policy changes and contact

If a future version introduces accounts, cloud sync, subscriptions, or new data practices, this page will be updated. Questions about this policy can be sent to victoriacheng1122@gmail.com.
